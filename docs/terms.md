## 8. Offline use and sync

The app works offline by design. Records are written to your device first and synced when a
connection is available.

Consequences you should understand:

- A record created offline exists only on that device until it syncs. Losing or wiping the device
  before it syncs loses that record.
- Two people editing the same record on different devices while offline will resolve to the last
  write that reaches the server.
- Receipt numbers issued offline are unique but may not be strictly consecutive across devices.
