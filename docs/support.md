## What to include

For anything billing-related, send the **order id** from Google Play and the **email address on the
account**. Without those we usually cannot identify the purchase.

## What we can and cannot help with

**We can** help with sign-in problems, sync issues, subscription and plan questions, data export, and
account deletion.

**We cannot** intervene in money between a study space and its members. We never receive, hold or
route those fees, so we cannot refund a member, reverse a payment, or settle a dispute between a
space and a student. Cancelling a payment inside the app is a bookkeeping correction to that space's
own ledger — it moves no money.

**If you are a student at a study space**, your records were entered by that space, not by us. Ask
them to correct or delete your details. See [Privacy Policy §15](privacy.md).

## Subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold through Google Play, which is the merchant of record. Cancel from
**Google Play → Subscriptions**. See [Refund & Cancellation](refunds.md).

## Deleting your account

See [Delete your account](delete-account.md). It can be done from inside the app, or by email if you
no longer have it installed.
