## 1. What this covers

This policy covers the **Study Spaces subscription** — what a space owner pays
`[PLACEHOLDER — registered legal entity name]` for member limits, staff seats and reporting. See also
the [Terms of Service](terms.md).

**It does not cover fees your members pay you.** That money never passes through us: it is paid to
you directly and the app only records it. Refunding a member is a matter between you and them, and we
have no standing in it. See §5.

## 2. How subscriptions are billed

Subscriptions are sold and charged through **Google Play Billing**. Google is the merchant of record.
Payment, renewal and refunds are handled under Google's terms and by Google's systems.

Plans renew automatically each billing period until cancelled.

## 3. Cancelling

Cancel any time from **Google Play → Subscriptions**, or from within the app where a link is provided.

**Cancellation stops the next renewal; it does not end the current period.** Your paid features stay
available until the period you have already paid for runs out, then the space returns to the free
tier. Nothing is deleted when a subscription ends — your records remain, and recording payments keeps
working. What changes is that plan limits apply again, so adding new members may be blocked until you
free capacity or resubscribe.

## 4. Refunds

**We do not offer pro-rata refunds for unused time.** Cancelling part-way through a period does not
refund the remainder.

We will consider a refund where:

- the service was materially unavailable for an extended period and the fault was ours;
- you were charged after cancelling, or charged twice for the same period;
- a plan was activated in error and not used.

Because Google is the merchant of record, **most refunds are requested through Google Play**, which
has its own refund window and process. If Google declines and you believe one of the cases above
applies, write to [shubhamkr.devapps@gmail.com](mailto:shubhamkr.devapps@gmail.com) with your order id and we will review it.

Refunds, when granted, go back to the original payment method. We cannot refund to a different
account.

## 5. Your members' fees are not our business

To be explicit, because this is the most common confusion:

- We never receive, hold or route the fees your members pay you.
- We cannot refund a member on your behalf, reverse a payment, or intervene in a dispute between you.
- Cancelling a payment inside the app is a **bookkeeping correction to your own ledger**. It moves no
  money and tells no bank anything.

If a member wants their money back, you refund them however you took it.
