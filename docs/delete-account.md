## Deleting from inside the app

1. Open **Study Spaces**
2. Go to **Settings**
3. Scroll to **Danger zone**
4. Tap **Delete my account**
5. Read what will be destroyed, type `DELETE` to confirm

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

## Deleting without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email [shubhamkr.devapps@gmail.com](mailto:shubhamkr.devapps@gmail.com) from the address
your account uses. We will verify it is you and complete the deletion within
**30 days**.

## What gets deleted

**Your account:** your sign-in identity, and your profile — name, email, phone and address.

**Every space you own, and everything in it:** members, fee payments, receipts, shifts, expenses,
monthly financial totals, device registrations and purchase records. This is permanent.

**Your access to spaces owned by others:** you are removed from them. The space itself and its
records are untouched — they belong to that owner, not to you.

## What is kept, and why

**Nothing of yours is retained after deletion.** If you want your fee history, receipts or reports,
**export them before you delete** — Reports offers CSV export, and we cannot recover anything
afterwards.

Copies may persist briefly in encrypted database backups, which roll off automatically within
**7 days**. They are not accessible in the running service.

## If you are a student at a study space

Your details were entered by the space you attend, not by us. **Ask that study space to delete your
record** — they control it and can erase it from within the app. If they do not respond, contact
[shubhamkr.devapps@gmail.com](mailto:shubhamkr.devapps@gmail.com) and we will assist them in responding, but we cannot act on
their data without their instruction.

## If your space has other staff

Deleting your account deletes the spaces you own, which removes access for every staff member in
them. Tell your team first.
